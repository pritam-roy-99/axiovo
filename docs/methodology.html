Litveyra data coverage and methodology

Litveyra is an independent, experimental open-source research workspace, not an official arXiv or SciRate service. This page documents what the site actually does and what it cannot guarantee.

Discover: two source scopes, one research focus

Latest arXiv uses a periodically generated local set of selected arXiv categories and curated historical IDs. Its index count is not the entire arXiv archive. Explore my field uses the public OpenAlex API to search works with an arXiv-hosted location and checks discipline/topic relevance. It can search far beyond the local snapshot but its results depend on OpenAlex coverage, source metadata, rate limits and classification. Missing papers in Litveyra are not evidence of absence from arXiv.

A single researcher focus controls main field, area and subtopic; starred interests are saved in the browser. The official arXiv search remains available for independent cross-checking.

Paper identifiers and version fidelity

An unversioned arXiv identifier links to the latest available revision on arxiv.org. A suffix such as v1 or v2 requests that exact revision, if it exists. OpenAlex's submitted/accepted/published manuscript classifications do not identify arXiv revision numbers. Publication dates in OpenAlex are not guaranteed to match the first arXiv submission date. Confirm priority, revisions and source PDFs on the original arXiv record.

Scientific relevance and AI

Field Explorer filters OpenAlex results by research field and textual topic evidence. If keyword results are sparse, a user-controlled OpenAlex embedding-based semantic search may suggest additional arXiv-hosted candidates, which are screened again. Semantic matching improves retrieval but does not establish that a candidate is relevant, novel, correct or equivalent to another theorem.

Research Assistant supports deterministic, source-linked abstract-level evidence and optional locally executed Qwen models (Ollama or experimental browser WebGPU). No hosted Litveyra general-purpose language model is activated. Model-generated interpretations and citations must be checked against the full original papers. Research Tools supports local PDF viewing and notes, comparisons, citation exploration and manual research update checks.

Library, profiles and collaboration

Saved arXiv IDs, specific revisions, paper notes, field interests, research questions and local researcher profiles live in browser storage unless the user separately configures an optional account/sync service. Clearing browser data can erase them. Export important reading and research data. Public comments and reactions currently rely on GitHub Issues; dedicated Litveyra social accounts depend on separately configured Supabase integration.

Updates and limitations

GitHub Actions rebuilds selected arXiv feeds and site assets on scheduled runs. These jobs may be delayed, fail or be paused. The daily OAI-PMH process adds a bounded subset of arXiv metadata, not a full historical mirror. Litveyra is a static GitHub Pages application and has no continuously running ingestion server. Full arXiv recall, authenticated community features and centrally synchronized research workspaces would require additional infrastructure.
