How to use Research Desk

Enter a title, keyword, author, DOI or arXiv ID, choose the archives, then select Search preprints. Open an original record to verify it. Add papers to comparison to compare two to five abstracts, save them to Library, or export the shortlist as RIS citations. Search coverage can be partial.
