HOW-TO VIDEO

Learn how to use Axiovo

Follow a real Axiovo browser session, step by step: choose an archive, search, filter, save papers, organize your Library and use Research Assistant. Includes narration and English captions.

Read video transcript
  1. On the Axiovo homepage, choose an archive or open Research Desk.
  2. In arXiv Discover, select an official subject, research topic and subtopic.
  3. Use the search field to look up keywords, a title, DOI or arXiv ID, and verify source records.
  4. In Research Desk, search arXiv, bioRxiv and ChemRxiv while keeping the source of each result visible.
  5. Expand advanced filters for authors, dates and sorting, and check whether data coverage is complete.
  6. Save useful papers to Library, use collections and notes, and follow authors.
  7. Ask a focused question in Research Assistant, then verify the original evidence.
  8. Use Settings and the Night/Day control to personalize Axiovo.
