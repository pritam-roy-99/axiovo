GUIDED TOUR

See a research session

From a first query to a source-checked shortlist. The illustrated tour includes narration and English captions.

Read video transcript
  1. Welcome to Axiovo. Search arXiv, bioRxiv, and ChemRxiv from a single research desk.
  2. Explore the subject directory of each archive. Search by category code, field name, or a subfield you have added.
  3. Enter a research question, a paper title, or an identifier. Use the date filters and check coverage separately for each archive.
  4. Open the original record, or add papers to a side-by-side comparison. Read their abstracts and export the citations you need.
  5. Save a search to rerun later, and keep papers from all three archives together in your library. Your searches and notes stay on this device.
  6. Open Research Tools for PDF annotation, or use the Research Assistant to investigate a paper. Always follow the evidence back to its source.
