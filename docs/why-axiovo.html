Why Axiovo?

Axiovo brings several preprint-discovery starting points into one free research workspace. Search for papers, follow links to original records, and keep track of your reading without needing an Axiovo account for the core local tools.

A source-first workflow

Discover

Explore metadata from arXiv, bioRxiv, ChemRxiv and Cryptology ePrint through archive pages and Research Desk. Sources have different coverage and refresh schedules.

Verify

Open each paper's original source. Axiovo's summaries, metadata and relevance rankings are aids to reading, not certification of scientific correctness or novelty.

Organize

Save papers, reading states and notes in a browser-local Library. Export important data for backup; it is not automatically synced between devices.

Inspect

Use Research Assistant for source-linked abstract inspection. Optional Ollama and experimental browser models depend on compatible hardware and setup.

How is it different from other research sites?

arXiv, bioRxiv, ChemRxiv and Cryptology ePrint are authoritative destinations for their own records. Google Scholar offers broad scholarly discovery, while ResearchGate focuses on researcher profiles and sharing. Axiovo does not replace any of these services or claim to index everything they contain.

Its focus is a combined, open-source workflow for preprint discovery and personal reading, with explicit links back to original sources. For exhaustive literature reviews, always check original databases and follow references independently.

Free, inspectable and independently developed

Axiovo is an independently maintained open-source project, not an official product of arXiv, the other archives, or OpenAI. Its core GitHub Pages tools are free. Optional local AI may require a separate model download and compatible hardware. Hosted inference is not provided unless a secure service is configured.
